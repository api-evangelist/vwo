Digital journeys are rarely linear. A visitor may land on your site from an email, browse anonymously, compare products across tabs, leave, return through search, and then show interest in a completely different category. Traditional personalization tools often struggle with this behavior because they rely on fixed segments, historical data, or known customer profiles.
